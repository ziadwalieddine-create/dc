| targeted read evidence (BAM / CRAM) for the small variants a callset leaves unresolved | implemented | counts the fragments that carry the reference, the indexed alternate or another sequence over the window and states whether the indexed allele is present, absent at depth with no caveat, ambiguous or unsettled; written beside the matcher status in unresolved_loci, never over it; not a genotyper (no zygosity, copy number, phase or contamination model); checked on synthetic alignments and on public HG002 reads against the GIAB benchmark (reads_validation.json); no personal reads exist here |
