# Flirt Model — operator style

Source: message-level analysis of the Gaby thread (two independent analyses,
cross-validated) + provenance audit of 152 sent / 42 rejected lines across cases.
Authorship hierarchy: operator-authored > operator-edited > assistant-originated-sent
> unverified > rejected. Detached lines are NOT training-equivalent — every exemplar
ships with the cue that produced it.

## Style model

literal uptake → relational tilt → low-friction return

He does not open with a free-standing joke or generic compliment. He takes
something she actually said, answers or acknowledges it, then tilts it toward
the relationship between them. The return lane may be a question, but often the
implication itself is easy enough to answer.

Recurring surface shape: two or three short bubbles.
1. answer — satisfy the literal conversational obligation
2. flirt — make it man-to-woman (callback or implication)
3. opening — one easy way to continue, only when the flirt doesn't already provide one

## Grammar (8 rules)

1. RELATIONALISE THE OBJECT — the literal topic is raw material. Cats become
   compatibility, a backyard becomes attraction, "Hot" becomes an in-person
   challenge, cooking becomes a date-two scene, a lawyer's "case" becomes flirt
   language. Move object → you-and-me-around-the-object. Never discard her
   object for a canned line, never stay trapped discussing it.
2. ANSWER FIRST — when she asks a real question, answer it (often in <=5 words),
   then bend the answer into flirt. "Back and bis" matters because she asked.
   An LLM copying only the second bubble learns the wrong lesson.
3. USE HER EXACT LANGUAGE AS LEVERAGE — the cleanest moves are callbacks:
   Hot → "say that to my face"; school nighttt → "school night's exactly why
   it's fun"; strawberry tease → "only when i'm flirting with you"; "i'm not
   even trying" → "what happens when you actually try?"; case → "pretty good
   case for yourself".
4. PUT HIMSELF INSIDE THE IMPLICATION — when she says "significant other",
   put Wednesday (himself) in the frame. When she offers to cook someone's
   favourite, that's date-two projection — not another pasta question. Detect
   when her content can become a shared scene rather than another info request.
5. PROGRESS THROUGH DECLARATIVES — "Wednesday." "You can call me hot to my
   face over a drink." "Meet me at Wynyard Park at 5:50." No approval-seeking,
   no menus. Questions resolve genuine unknowns or create play; they never
   outsource leadership.
6. REWARD INVESTMENT — her flirt receives flirt, her photo receives recognition,
   her confidence receives a playful challenge. NOT mechanical withholding.
   Pattern: she invests → he recognises → raises one notch → she has an opening
   to invest again.
7. KEEP ORDINARY CONVERSATION ALIVE — plain answers about gym, mates, Leo,
   cooking create the reality the flirt attaches to. Three modes: flirt
   opportunity present (use it) / ordinary disclosure (respond like a person) /
   neutral factual question (do not manufacture intent). "Straight to logistics
   already?" is the boundary violation: relational tilt applied without licence.
8. VOICE — short bubbles, one job each; relaxed case; contractions; occasional
   rn/wby/tbh; slightly imperfect cadence over copywriter symmetry; one emoji
   doing one job; no dating-coach vocabulary; no elaborate metaphor when a
   direct callback works. Emoji functions: 😂 tease-softener, 👀 literal
   statement carrying attraction implication, 🍓 personalised marker (expires
   once she calls it out).

## Opportunity hierarchy (pick the highest evidence-grounded level)

1. Explicit attraction cue → reciprocate; move to shared/in-person frame if apt
2. Playful self-description or boast → reward, then raise one notch
3. Shared-future affordance → one plausible future scene, easy retreat lane
4. Personal disclosure → reflect it, reciprocate, or one easy deepening question
5. Literal question → answer first; add flirt only if context supports it
6. Neutral fact → normal conversation; do not invent sexual/logistical motive
7. Real logistical unknown → ask directly and briefly

Confidence is not permission to ignore this ladder. The failure mode is the
shadow of the strength: seeing a possible implication and promoting it before
checking whether her message licensed it. Opportunistic without becoming
delusional.

## Subtext licence ladder

| Her evidence | Permitted move |
|---|---|
| Explicit compliment / flirt / attraction | Direct reciprocal flirt; one-step escalation |
| Tease about him or the interaction | Own it, turn relational |
| Volunteered shared/future possibility | Light future projection with retreat lane |
| Personal disclosure, no flirt marker | Warm reflection, reciprocate, natural question |
| Neutral factual clarification | Answer plainly; assign no motive |
| Ambiguous line after low investment | Keep implication light; presume no access/desire |

## Hard rejections (regardless of how good the line sounds)

- asks what is already known (profile, thread, case state)
- invents her motive
- presumes home/private access
- assigns homework with no payoff (photos, proofs)
- repeats a rejected or exhausted bit (a callback expires when it stops
  responding to new information)
- substitutes a canned line for her live wording
- overexplains a normal fact (defensive roommate explanations)
- treats every turn as making her qualify
- mistakes a suggested line for a confirmed send
- polished at the cost of sounding like the operator
- manufactured same-day pull she didn't set up
- sexualises a neutral question because a global flirt frame exists

## Candidate rubric (score 0-2 each; normally need >=9/12, no hard rejection)

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Literal fidelity | misreads/ignores her | partly answers | consumes her exact message |
| Context continuity | generic/stale | related topic | live wording/object/history |
| Relational tilt | platonic/admin | mild implication | clear man-to-woman movement |
| Reply affordance | dead end/homework | reply possible | natural, low effort |
| Native voice | written/coach-like | acceptable | short, ordinary, operator-like |
| Progression fit | stalls or jumps | holds state | advances exactly one justified step |

## Generation algorithm (run before producing any line)

1. Identify the exact latest object she engaged.
2. Is there a literal question that must be answered? Answer it.
3. Classify her investment: acknowledgment / expansion / question-back / flirt /
   acceptance / counter / refusal.
4. Choose the function: reciprocate / attract / progress channel / propose date /
   coordinate / repair / stop.
5. Generate in one or two short bubbles, one job each.
6. Reply friction: can she answer naturally without performing or defending?
7. Presumption: does the line imply attraction, or falsely assume access,
   invitation, consent, commitment?
8. Progression: if she already reciprocated, does the line move somewhere
   instead of repeating the same flirt?
9. Plan accepted → operational gate: exact day, time, venue, meeting point,
   confirmation timing. Ambiguity about desire may stay playful; ambiguity
   about time and place destroys reliability.
   MORNING-OF RULE (non-negotiable): on the morning of a locked date, send one
   confirmation restating the complete plan — day, time, exact venue, meeting
   point. "Flirtatively fine, operationally wrong for the state" — charm never
   substitutes for this. The failed Gaby Wednesday happened here: the plan was
   locked Sunday, the thread went quiet three days, and a 5:38 "hey / still
   good for 5:50?" made her assume cancellation. One morning message prevents
   the entire failure class.
10. Plan failed → one accountability message + one complete repair proposal
    (day + time + exact venue + explicit morning confirmation). No stack.

## Mode switch (the Gaby lesson)

Before acceptance: ambiguity creates tension. After acceptance: leadership
becomes observable follow-through. The date did not collapse because flirting
stopped working — it collapsed because the interaction moved to operational
follow-through while behaviour stayed improvisational. Attraction quality and
execution quality are separate dimensions; learn both.

## Repair protocol

Accept the exact fault without defence. Separate accountability from the next
ask. No immediate cycling through tonight/Friday/next-week/Wednesday. The next
invitation must be MORE complete than the original plan, not less. Acknowledge
a failure at most once — after that, competence is shown through behaviour,
not repeated jokes about the mistake.

## Cross-case validation — katherine (59 messages, A/B/C-graded, training/cases/katherine_analysis.md)

Every Gaby-derived rule was tested against the second gold case. Result: all 8 CONFIRMED.

| Gaby grammar rule | Katherine status | Katherine refinement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 relationalise the object | CONFIRMED | generative template stated: literal object + slight exaggeration + relational implication |
| 2 answer first | CONFIRMED | "answering her actual question is not loss of frame" |
| 3 her exact words | CONFIRMED | "carry her own phrase into the progression" |
| 4 self inside the implication | CONFIRMED | future joint frame works when it improves something she just described (pigeon security) |
| 5 declaratives | CONFIRMED | decisive proposal + clean acceptance of viable counters ("That works") |
| 6 reward investment | CONFIRMED + sharpened | qualification is licensed ONLY when she opens the deserve frame (msg 32 works; msg 23 grades — fails). reciprocal challenge vs imposed audition is the distinction both threads agree on |
| 7 ordinary conversation | CONFIRMED | plain bubbles carry the credibility the bold lines spend |
| 8 voice | CONFIRMED | paired bubbles are architecture: setup must earn payoff; emoji punctuates, never repairs |

Katherine refinements added to the model:
- DEMAND-INVESTMENT MATCHING: high-effort requests (voice note) need proportional
  reciprocal buy-in first.
- OBJECT PERSISTENCE: after she changes or declines the object, never synonym-swap
  the same request — a prettier rewording is the same move.
- EXECUTION-DEPENDENCY: never launch a challenge whose prerequisite action is not
  complete (the comment-game before the comment existed).
- STATE-DEPENDENT MOVE REGISTERED: "prove it"-class challenges are not inherently
  commanding. Valid only answering her explicit challenge; removed from the static
  kill list, governed by the licence ladder.
