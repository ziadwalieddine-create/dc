# Read

When this skill is triggered, or a woman is mentioned with a thread, the message in front of you is the context. Read it. Do not wait for her bubble, heat, or extension to be filled in by someone else.

`run_turn` takes that message. A line marked `she:` or `she said` is her bubble. His instruction in the same message sets the claim. The case file is what was already known. It is not a form that has to be finished before the read.

Her last bubble is the evidence. Commit. Move.

One dominant read, stored on the case, not printed under the line:

- `refuse` — she shut it. Stop. A tease in the same bubble loses.
- `dry` — a token or a bare hey. The bit stays. Amplitude 0. No apology, no restart.
- `escalate` — charge or a challenge. Heat 2. She opened it. Push that, not a new topic.
- `tease` — she touched the live bit and added something. Heat 2. Stay on it.
- `offer` — she named the move. Play it. Do not book it unless his claim is already a plan and the day is still ahead.
- `logistics` — she asked about a day and the window is not locked. Do not invent one.
- `question` — answer what she asked. If she also touched the bit, answer inside it.
- `withdraw` — busy, later, not sure, and no counter. Heat 0. Do not chase the same ask.
- `topic` — a live bit exists and this bubble is not it. Trade on what she actually said. Leave the old bit live.
- `invest` — new specific, no bit yet. Use it.
- `none` — she has not written. Do not invent a bubble.

`confidence` is `high`, or `split` when a challenge and a withdrawal are in the same breath. Split still picks the challenge. The `reversal` is the one fact that would kill the read. It is not a second opinion and it is not shown to her.

Heat is 0, 1, or 2. Not a maybe. A refusal, a null claim, and an unconfirmed send still stop the action. Those are evidence, not nerves.
