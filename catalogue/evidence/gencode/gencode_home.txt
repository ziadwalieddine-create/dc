 The goal of the GENCODE project is to identify and classify all gene features
 in the human and mouse genomes with high accuracy based on biological evidence,
 and to release these annotations for the benefit of biomedical research and
 genome interpretation.
 GENCODE now offers a first catalog of human promoter windows .
 The GENCODE human and mouse lncRNA annotations are significantly expanding as we
 integrate models from our Capture Long-read Sequencing project .
 The GENCODE Primary transcript set captures a minimal set of transcripts at protein coding genes.
 GENCODE are supporting the annotation of non-canonical human ORFs predicted by Ribo-seq data,
 now including the integration of peptidomics and immunopeptidomics data.
