ClinPGx/PharmGKB data

PharmGKB (now ClinPGx) is a NIH sponsored research project (U24HG010615) funded to collect, encode, and disseminate knowledge about the impact of human genetic variations on drug response. The knowledge and data covered by this agreement include and are not limited to research variant annotations from the literature, clinical interpretations of these variants, gene summaries for very important pharmacogenes (VIPs), pathway and network diagrams, relationships between gene, drugs and diseases and all software that support the ClinPGx/PharmGKB.
