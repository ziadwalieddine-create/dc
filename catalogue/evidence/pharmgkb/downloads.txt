Downloads

ClinPGx data and knowledge are available as download files. We have found that it is often critical for users to check with our team at feedback@clinpgx.org
(opens in new window)
 before embarking on a large project using these data to be sure that the files and data we make available are being interpreted correctly. We generally do not need to be a co-author on such analyses; we just want to make sure that there is a correct understanding of our data before lots of resources are spent.
