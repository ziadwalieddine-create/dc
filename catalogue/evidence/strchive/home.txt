An archive of STRs associated with human diseases

Loci
Paper

Short Tandem Repeats (STRs) are a type of genetic variation that are associated with many rare diseases. Information about pathogenic STRs is often out-of-date and scattered across different databases, making it difficult to find and interpret STR variants. STRchive aims to solve this problem by providing a central community resource.
